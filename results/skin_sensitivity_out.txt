WHAT THIS MEANS, STATED PLAINLY

   At the fouled 8.0 K the package reports, the wall is at 6 cycles and the
   best feasible point is 5 cycles, worth 6.25 % on cycles alone.

   At a clean-surface 3.0 K, the wall moves to 6 cycles and the best feasible
   point is 5 cycles, worth 6.25 % on cycles alone.

   The pre-registered 15 % water criterion needs 8.5 cycles.
      dT = 2.4 K -> last feasible 5 cycles -> 15 % is unreachable
      dT = 3.0 K -> last feasible 5 cycles -> 15 % is unreachable
      dT = 3.7 K -> last feasible 5 cycles -> 15 % is unreachable
      dT = 5.0 K -> last feasible 5 cycles -> 15 % is unreachable
      dT = 8.0 K -> last feasible 5 cycles -> 15 % is unreachable

   MECHANISM, measured rather than remembered.

        T_C  SI_gypsum    D-H A   (limit 0.3)
         36    -0.1684   0.5225   ok
         40    -0.1758   0.5267   ok
         44    -0.1816   0.5310   ok
         48    -0.1861   0.5355   ok
         50    -0.1878   0.5378   ok

   SI_gypsum has an interior MINIMUM at 59.5 C (SI -0.1914), so it is retrograde below that point and
   prograde above it. The condenser skin in these runs sits near
   40 C -- on the flat bottom of that curve, which is why the
   sweep below moves so little.

   RESULT: across the whole band dT = 2.4 to 8.0 K, clean to fouled,
   the gypsum wall does NOT move. It is 6 cycles at every skin
   temperature tested, and the last feasible count is 5 at every one.

   That is a change of status for this open item, and it cuts FOR
   the package rather than against it. The hardcoded 8 K was the
   least-defended input in the chemistry chain precisely because
   the ceilings were believed to be proportional to it. Under the
   corrected gypsum solubility they are not sensitive to it at all
   over the range the film calculation admits.

   The 8 K literal should still be replaced by a call to
   wall_bulk_delta_t -- an assumption that happens not to bind is
   still an assumption -- but it is no longer load-bearing for
   either ceiling, and the reported saturation violations are no
   longer conditional on a fouled condenser.

   The 15 % water criterion needs 8.5 cycles and the wall is at 6, so it stays
   unreachable at every skin temperature in the band. V5 is not rescored.
